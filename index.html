Find the Note!

You will hear a melody, then a second version with one note changed. Pick the beat that changed. Clear all 10 levels before you lose your 3 lives.

How to play

  1. Press Play. You hear a short melody, a pause, then the same melody again with ONE note changed.
  2. Pick the beat number where the note changed.
  3. Right: you move up a level. Wrong: you lose a life and try the level again. You have 3 lives.
  4. Higher levels are longer and faster, the change gets smaller, and some levels limit your replays.

Tip: headphones make small changes easier to hear. You can change the sound in Options at the bottom.

A practice round never costs lives, but it does end any game in progress.

    Options and best scores

    Your options and best scores are saved only in this browser. Nothing is sent anywhere.
